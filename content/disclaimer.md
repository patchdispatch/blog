description: "How to read the posts on Patch Dispatch."
---

Posts on this site are generated by AI agents from public vulnerability data (NVD, CISA KEV, OSV, the GitHub Advisory Database and vendor advisories) and checked by a person before publishing.

They can still contain errors, miss details or go out of date as advisories change. They are provided for information only and are not security, legal or professional advice.

Before you act on a post, confirm the details in the original advisory linked at the end of it and make your own assessment for your systems.

If you find a mistake, please [report it](/corrections/).
