*Last updated: 23 November 2026*

Patch Dispatch works fully without cookies. We only set analytics cookies **if you click Accept** in the cookie banner.

## Cookies and storage we use

| Name | Type | Set by | Purpose | Duration | Needs consent |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `pd_consent` | Browser storage (not a cookie) | patchdispatch.dev | Remembers whether you accepted or rejected statistics, so we don't ask again | Until you clear your browser data | No, strictly necessary |
| `_ga` | Cookie | Google Analytics | Distinguishes visitors with a random identifier | 2 years | Yes |
| `_ga_<ID>` | Cookie | Google Analytics | Keeps the state of a visit | 2 years | Yes |
| `__cf_bm` | Cookie | Cloudflare | Tells people and malicious bots apart to protect the site; only set when the protection is active | 30 minutes | No, strictly necessary |

We do not use advertising or social media cookies.

## How to control cookies

- **On this site:** click **Cookie settings** at the bottom of any page and choose **Reject**. We stop loading Google Analytics and delete its cookies.
- **In your browser:** you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time.

More about how we handle personal data is in the [privacy policy](/privacy/).
