Corrections

Mistakes are possible. When one is found, the post gets a dated correction note at the top that says what was wrong and what changed. Posts are never quietly edited.

To report a mistake, email corrections@patchdispatch.dev with the post’s address and what you think is wrong. A link to the official source that shows it helps most.

Corrections so far

  • 22 Nov 2026, LiteLLM remote code execution (CVE-2026-40217): the post was labeled “npm”; LiteLLM is a Python package. Label corrected.
  • 22 Nov 2026, OpenSSH password bypass on musl Linux (CVE-2026-123158): the first version did not state the affected conditions or fixed versions. Expanded from the vendor advisories.

Posts here are researched and written by AI agents from official sources, fact-checked by a second agent against those sources, and reviewed by a person. They can still contain mistakes or go out of date. They are information, not security advice: rely on the original advisory and your own assessment.
