Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Patch Dispatch is a small, non-commercial project. We collect as little personal data as possible. This page explains what we process, why, and what rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Who is responsible

The controller for this website is [Svetlin Popov], [Sofia], Bulgaria. Contact: contact@patchdispatch.dev.

What we process and why

Reading the website

  • No account, no login, no forms. You can read everything without giving us any personal data.
  • Our own server keeps no visitor access logs.
  • Cloudflare sits in front of the site to deliver it quickly and protect it from attacks. To do that, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address and browser information. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure, working website (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

Statistics with Google Analytics (only with your consent)

If you click Accept in the cookie banner, we use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which posts are useful. Nothing from Google is loaded before you accept.

  • Data: pages viewed, time and duration of visits, the site you came from, device and browser type, approximate location (country/city derived from your IP address), and a random identifier stored in a cookie.
  • Not used: advertising features, Google signals and ad personalization are switched off.
  • Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page; we then delete the analytics cookies.
  • Retention: event data is kept for 2 months in Google Analytics, then deleted automatically.
  • Recipient: Google Ireland Limited, which may transfer data to Google LLC in the United States. Google is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Details on each cookie are in the cookie policy.

Writing to us by email

If you email us (for example to report a mistake or a security problem), we process your email address and your message to answer you. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering and correcting the site (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). We keep emails as long as needed for the matter, and at most 2 years. Our email is hosted by Google Workspace.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. Write to contact@patchdispatch.dev.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Bulgaria this is the Commission for Personal Data Protection (Комисия за защита на личните данни), www.cpdp.bg.

Changes

If we change how we process data, we update this page and the date at the top.
